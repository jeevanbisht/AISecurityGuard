# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, assume good intent, and focus on
what is best for the community. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub Security Advisories or
to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
